Prism-MoE: Efficient Dense-to-MoE Conversion for Visual Autoregressive Generation

ICML 2026 · Generative · MoE
TUE · Jul 7 · 2:00–3:45 PM  |  Hall A · Poster #2611

Transforms pretrained dense visual autoregressive models into sparse MoE models at low cost: trajectory-consistent initialization casts expert init as a principled decomposition that preserves the pretrained generation trajectory, and confidence-adaptive sparse fine-tuning aligns expert specialization with visual token information density. Under 10% of the standard training budget, dense-level quality with only 37.5% active parameters.

Ying Li, Zefang Wang, Zhaode Wang, Zhiwen Chen, Chengfei Lv, Huan Wang

02

ARC-Decode: Accelerated Decoding with Risk-Bounded Acceptance

ICML 2026 · Speculative Decoding
TUE · Jul 7 · 2:00–3:45 PM  |  Hall A · Poster #2815

Speculative decoding under sampling (T>0) over-rejects low-risk drafts, so real speedups lag greedy decoding. ARC-Decode is a training-free relaxed-acceptance rule: a Jensen–Shannon-divergence risk bound plus confidence-based pre-verification accepts drafts that provably preserve the target distribution. Plugged into EAGLE-3, up to 1.6× end-to-end speedup under sampling with negligible quality change.

Ying Li, Zhaode Wang, Zhiwen Chen, Chengfei Lv, Huan Wang

03

RLKV: Which Heads Matter for Reasoning? RL-Guided KV Cache Compression

ICML 2026 · KV Cache Compression
TUE · Jul 7 · 10:30 AM–12:15 PM  |  Hall A · Poster #1610

Reasoning LLMs are fragile to KV cache compression during long chain-of-thought generation. RLKV uses reinforcement-learning probing to identify the small set of attention heads that keep reasoning coherent, giving them full KV cache while aggressively compressing the rest — 20–50% cache reduction at near-lossless accuracy and up to 1.21× speedup.

Wenjie Du, Li Jiang, Keda Tao, Xue Liu, Huan Wang
▸ICML ▸Webpage ▸ArXiv ▸Code
04

SparseSSM: Efficient Selective Structured State Space Models Can Be Pruned in One-Shot

ICML 2026 · Pruning · SSM
WED · Jul 8 · 2:30–4:15 PM  |  Hall A · Poster #1014

The first training-free pruning framework extending optimal brain surgeon (OBS) to state-space architectures: a second-order saliency score aggregates Hessian-trace information across time steps for the time-shared SSM transition matrix, with component sensitivity analysis guiding FFN pruning. Prunes 50% of Mamba SSM weights with no zero-shot accuracy loss and no fine-tuning — state-of-the-art for Mamba-based LLMs.

Kaiwen Tuo, Huan Wang
