Huan Wang

Welcome to my homepage!👋🏻 My name is Huan Wang (Chinese: 王欢; my first name means "happiness or joy" in Chinese). I work for (myself at) Westlake University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor (助理教授, 特聘研究员, 博导, PI). I lead a lab called ENCODE Lab (高效智能计算实验室).

I did my Ph.D. (2024) at Northeastern University (Boston, USA), advised by Prof. Yun (Raymond) Fu at SMILE Lab. Before that, I earned my M.S. and B.E. degrees at Zhejiang University (Hangzhou, China), advised by Prof. Haoji Hu. I also visited VLLab at UC Merced, luckily working with Prof. Ming-Hsuan Yang. I was fortunate to intern at Google/Snap/MERL/Alibaba, working with many fantastic industrial researchers. I serve as an Area Chair for AAAI/ICLR/CVPR/NeurIPS 2026.

My research is focused on Efficient AI and AI Evaluation. Efficient AI is an area trying to build efficient and reliable AI systems that are scalable and self-improving. AI Evaluation aims to evaluate the capability (and limits) of AI models, trying to answer some fundemental questions, e.g., what is intelligence, how to measure it. The ultimate goal of my research is to understand the nature of Intelligence, and ensure the scientific advances promote human goodness.

I also spent some time on a few philosophical problems about AI (or not), where I have been inspired by Kant, Duchamp.
