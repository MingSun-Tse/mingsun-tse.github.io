Huan Wang

Welcome to my homepage!👋🏻 My name is Huan Wang (Chinese: 王欢. My first name means "happiness or joy" in Chinese). I work for (myself at) Westlake University(map-in-Chinese) (School of Engineering, Department of Artificial Intelligence) as a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor (特聘研究员). I did my Ph.D. (2024) at Northeastern University (Boston, USA), advised by Prof. Yun (Raymond) Fu at SMILE Lab. Before that, I did my M.S. and B.E. at Zhejiang University (Hangzhou, China), advised by Prof. Haoji Hu. I also visited VLLab at University of California, Merced, luckily working with Prof. Ming-Hsuan Yang. I was fortunate to intern at Google / Snap / MERL / Alibaba, working with many fantastic industrial researchers. I am an AC for AAAI/ICLR/CVPR 2026.

I am interested in a variety of problems in AI. Our group, ENCODE Lab, is committed to building efficient and reliable AI systems that are scalable and self-improving, mainly focusing on three areas: efficient AI, multimodal learning, and generative AI, with interdisciplinary research on AI for Environment, which aims to use AI to solve environmental and sustainability problems for our planet. My past research spans many tasks in vision and language modeling:

  • high-level vision: image classification, detection, etc. [GReg, PaI, TPP];
  • low-level vision: image restoration [ASSL, SRP, ISS-P], stylization [NST], snapshot compressive imaging [QuantizedSCI, MobileSCI];
  • 3D reconstruction and rendering, NeRF: [R2L, MobileR2L,LightAvatar];
  • image generation with diffusion models or AR models: [SnapFusion, ARPG];
  • LLMs / MLLMs: [DyCoke, HoliTom, Survey].

I also spent some time on some philosophical problems about AI (or not), where I have been inspired by Kant, Duchamp.
